**6. What do you do in detail?**
Legacy Building Management Systems (BMS) are fragmented and purely reactive, causing massive energy waste and alarm fatigue for facility managers. ARVIS acts as an intelligent "Brain" layer over existing infrastructure (like BACnet).

In detail, ARVIS does three things:
1.  **AI Ops Copilot:** Facility managers can query building status in natural language (English/Arabic) instead of navigating complex dashboards.
2.  **Predictive Maintenance:** Our ML engines (XGBoost/Isolation Forests) analyze equipment data to predict failures (e.g., chillers) up to 7 days in advance.
3.  **Real-time ROI Calculation:** The system autonomously identifies energy waste (like simultaneous heating/cooling) and explicitly calculates the QAR savings of fixing it.

ARVIS is fundamentally different because it possesses deep vertical integration with actual industrial protocols (BACnet, Modbus). We utilize a proprietary Hybrid LLM router—separating logical reasoning from fast tool execution—meaning ARVIS can securely interact with critical building infrastructure without hallucinations. Furthermore, our native Arabic LLM support and built-in GSAS sustainability reporting tools make it uniquely tailored for the MENA market.
