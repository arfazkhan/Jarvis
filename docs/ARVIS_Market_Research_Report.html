Executive Summary

ARVIS Ops Copilot is positioned at a critical intersection in the commercial real estate technology market: facility teams have access to massive volumes of operational data but lack the reasoning and interpretation required to act on it effectively.

Core Value Proposition

Rather than replacing existing Building Management Systems, ARVIS functions as a vendor-neutral advisory layer that translates raw facility data into human-readable insights. Facility teams can query their buildings in natural language, receive explainable recommendations with confidence levels, and maintain full control over operational decisions.
