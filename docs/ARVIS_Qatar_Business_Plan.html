Executive Summary

The Opportunity

Qatar has invested over $200 billion in infrastructure for the 2022 World Cup and beyond. These buildings now need intelligent management to optimize energy, reduce costs, and meet Qatar Vision 2030 sustainability goals. ARVIS provides the AI-powered "brain" that transforms buildings from passive structures into intelligent, responsive environments.

What is ARVIS?

ARVIS (Advanced Responsive Virtual Intelligence System) is an AI-powered building management platform that:

  • Understands natural language — Building operators speak to ARVIS in Arabic or English
  • Learns and adapts — AI learns building patterns and optimizes automatically
  • Reduces energy costs — 25-40% energy savings through intelligent control
  • Predicts problems — Detects equipment issues before failures occur
  • Integrates with existing systems — Works with Honeywell, Siemens, and other BMS

Risk Analysis

Market Entry Risk

Difficulty gaining initial customers in relationship-driven market.

Mitigation: Local partner (brother), SI partnerships, pilot programs at reduced rates.

Technical Risk

Integration challenges with existing BMS systems.

Mitigation: BACnet is industry standard; ARVIS already supports it. Extensive testing before launch.

Competition Risk

Large vendors (Honeywell, Siemens) adding AI features.

Mitigation: Focus on SMB segment they ignore. Arabic-first, lower cost, faster deployment.

Regulatory Risk

Certifications or approvals required for building systems.

Mitigation: ARVIS is software layer, not safety-critical hardware. Partner with certified SIs for installation.

Execution Risk

Delays in hiring, product development, or customer acquisition.

Mitigation: Milestone-based funding release. Conservative projections. Multiple go-to-market channels.
