Executive Summary

Commercial buildings today are rich in data but poor in operational intelligence.

Most large buildings already run Building Management Systems (BMS) from established vendors. These systems collect vast amounts of sensor data, alarms, schedules, and energy information. What they do not provide is reasoning — an understanding of what matters right now, why it is happening, and what should be done next.

The ARVIS Approach

ARVIS Ops Copilot is a vendor-neutral, advisory AI layer that sits on top of existing BMS infrastructure. It helps facility teams interpret what is happening in their buildings, reduce operational noise, and make better decisions — without replacing or interfering with existing systems.

ARVIS is intentionally designed as a decision-support system, not an autonomous controller. This makes it safe, explainable, and suitable for deployment in existing commercial buildings in conservative environments such as Qatar.

The Problem

Despite years of investment in BMS platforms, commercial building operations still face persistent issues:

  • Hundreds of alarms with no prioritisation or context
  • Comfort complaints that repeat without clear root causes
  • Energy inefficiencies discovered only after monthly bills
  • Multiple vendor dashboards that do not communicate
  • Heavy reliance on a few experienced operators and tribal knowledge

"The core problem is not lack of data. The core problem is lack of interpretation and reasoning across systems."

Facility teams are forced to manually connect dots across alarms, trends, schedules, and complaints — a process that does not scale and breaks down as buildings and portfolios grow.

Pain Point Reality Cost Impact
Alarm Fatigue 95% of alarms are noise, ignored by operators Critical issues missed
Root Cause Analysis Takes 2-3 days to trace recurring complaints Tenant dissatisfaction

Product Overview

5.1 BMS Connectivity (Read-Only)

ARVIS connects to existing infrastructure via standard protocols:

  • BACnet/IP — Industry standard for commercial HVAC
  • Modbus — For legacy and industrial equipment
  • REST APIs — Where vendors expose modern interfaces

It reads sensor data, alarms, setpoints, schedules, and energy metrics. No control commands are issued in Phase 1.

5.2 Intelligent Alarm & Event Reasoning

Instead of raw alerts flooding the operator's screen, ARVIS provides:

  • Alarm clustering based on root causes
  • Priority ranking based on operational impact
  • Plain-language explanations with confidence scores

"3 cooling complaints on Floor 3 are likely caused by a stuck VAV damper in zone 3-East, not a chiller issue. Recommendation: Check damper actuator. Confidence: 87%"

5.3 Energy & Efficiency Insights

ARVIS analyses historical and live data to identify:

  • Abnormal energy usage patterns
  • After-hours wastage (HVAC running empty)
  • Cooling and heating inefficiencies
  • Zone-level anomalies

Insights are explained in operational terms, not just charts.

5.4 Natural Language Interface

Facility teams interact using English or Arabic:

  • "Why is energy higher this week?"
  • "Which zones are overcooled right now?"
  • "What caused yesterday's complaints on Floor 5?"

Responses are contextual, explainable, and logged for audit.

5.5 Reporting & Compliance

  • Energy summaries (daily, weekly, monthly)
  • Operational insight reports
  • GSAS-aligned sustainability exports
  • PDF and Excel formats for management

Safety, Trust & Governance

Principle Implementation
Advisory-Only Mode Phase 1 is read-only; no control commands issued
Human-in-the-Loop All recommendations require human approval
Confidence Indicators Every insight shows confidence % and reasoning
Full Audit Trail All queries and responses logged for compliance
