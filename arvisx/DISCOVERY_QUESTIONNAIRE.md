# ArvisX — Community Discovery Questionnaire

**Who you're talking to:** Secretary / Managing Committee member / Facility chairperson — owns
operations, budget, and resident complaints. Usually NOT technical.

**Your goal in this meeting:** understand how the building runs, quantify the pain, learn what
equipment and data exist, and find out who signs off. **Do NOT demo or pitch features until Section H.**
Let them describe the pain in their own words first.

**Opener (sets the frame):**
> "Before I show you anything, I'd like to understand how the building actually runs day to day —
> what eats your time, what generates complaints, where money leaks. Mind if I ask a bunch of questions?"

---

## A. Their role & the building (warm-up, ~5 min)

| Question | What you're really learning |
|---|---|
| How many towers / units / floors? Occupied vs sold? | Scale → pricing tier, pump/tank zoning |
| How long have you been on the committee? Voluntary or paid? | Champion's tenure + skin in the game |
| Walk me through a typical week running this place — what takes the most time? | Lets *them* name the pain first |
| Who does the hands-on work — in-house staff or an outsourced FM company? | Buyer vs user; who you'll train |

## B. The infrastructure (what to wire — scoping)

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| Where does water come from — municipal, borewell, tankers? One sump or several? Roof tanks? Booster pumps for high floors? | Confirms the **shared-system** model ArvisX fits; maps water assets |
| Diesel generators for backup power? How many, how big? | Power-backup service |
| Sewage treatment plant (STP) on site? Swimming pool? | STP + pool services |
| Fire panel and fire pumps — who monitors those today? | Fire = advisory-only line (state clearly later) |
| **Is there a piped cooking-gas system** — a basement bank feeding apartments? | The gas hook — strong if yes |
| Any building management system (BMS) already, or is each system standalone? | Standalone/fragmented → **your ICP**. Full BMS → different conversation |

## C. How it's run today (manual-process pain surface)

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| How do you find out a pump or generator has a problem — who tells you, and when? | Usually "when it fails / a resident calls" = reactive = your wedge |
| Is there a **daily checklist** the staff fill in? Can I see a recent one? | The pencil-whipping pain — ask to *see* it |
| Honestly — do you trust those readings, or do they get filled in at week-end from memory? | Names the integrity gap directly; watch their reaction |
| When something breaks, how do you track that it got fixed? WhatsApp, register, nothing? | Work-order gap |
| How do residents raise complaints today? Where do those go? | Resident-request loop |

## D. The cost of the pain (ROI ammunition — get numbers)

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| Last time a pump/generator failed unexpectedly — repair cost, and the resident fallout? | Failure-exposure number |
| Roughly the monthly diesel / electricity / water bill? | Waste-reduction framing |
| How many staff are involved in monitoring and meter reading? | Labour cost being automated |
| What % of your time goes to firefighting vs planning? | Time = the emotional pain |

## E. The two sharp hooks (test resonance — known pains)

**Gas billing:**
- "How is each apartment's cooking-gas usage billed today?"
- "Who walks around reading meters at month-end? How long does it take, and do residents ever dispute the bill?"
  *(Manual monthly meter round → strong, concrete hook.)*

**Checklist integrity:**
- "If I asked you to prove the pump room was actually checked last Tuesday, could you?"
- "Has a missed check ever turned into an expensive failure?"

## F. Data & systems readiness (commissioning feasibility)

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| Do these machines have digital sensors/meters, or all manual gauges? | Edge-hardware scope before learning can start |
| Reliable wifi / internet near the pump room, generator room, gas plant? | Connectivity for the gateway |
| Does everyone — committee, staff, residents — already use WhatsApp? | Confirms the delivery channel |
| Do you have an asset list / equipment register, even on paper? | Speeds commissioning |

## G. Decision & money (qualifies the deal — don't skip)

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| If you wanted to spend on something like this, who approves it — you, the full committee, the AGM? | Decision path + sales-cycle length |
| Is there a maintenance/capex budget, or is everything case-by-case? | Budget reality |
| What would this need to do in the first month for you to call it worth it? | Their success metric, in their words |
| Tried any software for this before? What happened? | Past failures / objections to preempt |

## H. The pilot ask (only now — after you've listened)

- "Based on what you've described, the pieces that would help you most are **X and Y**. Would you be open
  to a **30–60 day pilot on one tower** — no rip-and-replace, read-only, where I wire up your existing
  equipment and you start getting a daily WhatsApp summary?"
- "Who from your side would be the day-to-day contact — you, or the FM/technician?"

---

## Cheat sheet — read the room

**🟢 Green lights (strong fit):**
- Fragmented standalone systems, no BMS
- Manual gas-meter billing with disputes
- Checklist exists but nobody trusts it
- Reactive ("we find out when it breaks")
- Frustrated, tenured champion with budget authority
- Everyone on WhatsApp

**🔴 Red flags (deprioritize or rescope):**
- Full modern BMS already monitoring everything → advisory-only, smaller value
- No internet near plant rooms → hardware project before software value
- Pure volunteer committee, no budget, decisions only at annual AGM → 12-month cycle
- Fire monitoring is their #1 ask → regulatory/liability line; position alongside, never as system of record

**Say these out loud (build trust):**
- "It's **read-only** — it never controls your equipment, only advises."
- "Fire stays with your certified panel; we add visibility alongside it, we don't replace it."
- "There's a learning period — it watches for a few weeks before it flags anything, so it doesn't cry wolf."
