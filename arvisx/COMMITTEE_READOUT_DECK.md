### Slide 2 — The problem we set out to solve
- [Community] runs water, power-backup, STP, pool and fire-pump on separate systems with
  **no unified view** — tracked on WhatsApp, paper, and the FM's memory.
- Issues are noticed **after** they bite (dry tank, generator won't start, pump burnout).
- *Question we asked:* can one advisory layer make the building measurably better-run?

---

### Slide 3 — What ArvisX did (in one picture)
- Sat **on top** of your existing equipment (read-only — never controlled anything).
- Learned each asset's normal, then watched 24/7.
- Spoke to you in **WhatsApp**, in plain operations language — not raw sensor numbers.
- *Visual:* sensors → ArvisX → daily digest + alerts to the committee/FM.
