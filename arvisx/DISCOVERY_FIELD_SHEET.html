ArvisX Discovery — Field Sheet

Residential community ops intelligence · pilot discovery
Community: Contact / role: Date: Phone / WhatsApp:
Opener: “Before I show you anything — how does the building actually run day to day? What eats your time, what generates complaints, where does money leak?”  Listen first. Pitch last.

A. The building

  • Towers / units / floors; occupied vs sold?
  • On committee how long? Voluntary or paid?
  • Biggest time-sink in a typical week?
  • Hands-on work: in-house staff or outsourced FM?

C. How it's run today

  • How do you find out a pump/generator has a problem?
  • Daily checklist exist? Can I see one? ☐ y ☐ n — trusted, or week-end from memory?
  • How is a repair tracked to completion?
  • How do residents raise complaints?

D. Cost of the pain (get numbers)

  • Last unexpected failure — repair cost + resident fallout?
  • Monthly diesel / electricity / water bill?
  • Staff involved in monitoring + meter reading?
  • Firefighting vs planning — rough % of your time?

E. Two sharp hooks

  • Gas: how is each apartment billed? Who reads meters month-end? Disputes?
  • Checklist: could you prove the pump room was checked last Tuesday? Ever caused a failure?

F. Readiness

  • Digital sensors/meters or all manual gauges?
  • ☐ Internet near pump / generator / gas rooms   ☐ Everyone on WhatsApp
  • Asset list / equipment register (even paper)?

G. Decision & money

  • Who approves spend — you / committee / AGM?
  • Maintenance or capex budget?
  • “What would month one need to deliver to be worth it?”
  • Tried software before? What happened?
