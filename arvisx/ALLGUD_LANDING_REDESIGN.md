# AllGud — Landing Page Redesign

> External brand: **AllGud** · Internal: ArvisX · Market: India (INR) · Audience: facility managers, RWA/society committees, owners/developers.
> This doc fixes the current landing page's core problem — it sells two contradictory products — and gives a launch-ready, reordered flow with copy.

---

## 1. The one decision everything hangs on

The current page mixes two incompatible promises:

- **Foresight tool** — "know before it breaks", "stay in complete control" ✅ *(what AllGud actually is)*
- **Autopilot / dispatch service** — "automated vendor dispatching", "let us handle it" ❌ *(not built; product is read-only by design and never actuates)*

**Decision: AllGud is a foresight / readiness-intelligence platform. Read-only. It never dispatches, controls, or "handles" operations — it tells you what's failing, why, and what it'll cost, so your team acts first.**

Read-only is a **feature, not a limitation**: a committee that fears losing control trusts a tool that informs over one that acts. It's also the truthful, demoable, lower-liability position (critical near fire/safety systems).

---

## 2. Message spine (repeat this everywhere)

> **For** facility managers & committees **who** firefight surprise failures, **AllGud** gives one live readiness score — what's failing, **why**, and what it'll **cost** — delivered on WhatsApp, **so you act first and stay in control.**

**Differentiators, in reveal order:**
`One score → + the cost of each risk → + why it's failing → on WhatsApp → you stay in control`

**Voice:** calm, premium, plain-spoken. Operational language, never raw telemetry. Money in ₹. Confidence without hype.
